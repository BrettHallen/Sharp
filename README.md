## [Sharp PC-1350](/Sharp_PC-1350)
The Sharp PC-1350 is the smaller sibling of the PC-2500.  It has the same screen resolution and BASIC but doesn't have the built-in phone book and business programs ... nor the plotter and cassette interface (these require a dock like the CE-125).

## [Sharp PC-2500](/Sharp_PC-2500)
The Sharp PC-2500 (export) and PC-2500S (domestic Japanese) were a development of the PC-1350 Pocket Computer in an A4-form factor with a full-size QWERTY keyboard and built-in 4-colour/pen plotter/printer.
